#### Education

Chris received a BS in Psychology in 2019 from Loyola University Chicago, where he studied how social context affects the way groups perceive and process information. In high school, he completed student nursing rotations in the Pediatric Oncology, Pediatric Intensive Care, and Outpatient Phlebotomy units at Bronson Methodist Hospital in Kalamazoo, Michigan.

#### Research Interests

Chris is interested in the psychological, human factors, and human-computer interaction elements of mHealth research. He conducts studies of user experiences with sensing devices and runs focus group sessions with clinical providers to glean insights from clinical communities of practice that are relevant to the design and function of proposed mHealth systems.
